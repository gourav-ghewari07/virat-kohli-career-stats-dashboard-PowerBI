Virat Kohli Career Statistics Dashboard Insights

Executive Summary

This dashboard provides a detailed analysis of Virat Kohli's cricket career across Test, ODI, and T20 formats. The analysis highlights batting consistency, scoring efficiency, milestone achievements, and format-wise performance.
